# Directory profile copy

Ready to paste. Do these in the order listed. The whole set takes about two hours and it is
the highest-value free work available, because these directories outrank small law firm
websites in Google.

**Rule for all of them:** the business name, address, and phone must match the website
exactly, character for character. Google cross-checks them. "Suite 248" on one and "Ste 248"
on another weakens every listing you own.

The canonical set:

```
Hogan and Crown Law
9330 Lyndon B. Johnson Fwy
Lake Highland Towers, Suite 248
Dallas, TX 75243
469-288-1606
info@hoganandcrownlaw.com
https://hoganandcrownlaw.com
```

## 1. Google Business Profile — do this first

Go to `google.com/business`. Claim or create the listing.

**Primary category:** Immigration attorney
**Secondary categories:** Attorney, Legal services
**Service area:** add "United States" as well as Dallas. Immigration is federal, so the
national service area is accurate and it widens who can find you.

**Description** (fits the 750 character limit):

> Hogan and Crown Law is a United States immigration practice based in Dallas and acting for
> clients in every state. We handle family petitions, green cards, citizenship, asylum,
> deportation defence, work visas, and employer compliance.
>
> Queenette Hogan is admitted in New York and in Nigeria, with fourteen years in practice and
> a masters in corporate and financial law. Immigration law is federal, so we represent
> clients wherever they live in the US and at consulates abroad.
>
> The first fifteen minutes are free and there is no pitch at the end. You get an honest read
> of your options, the risks said out loud, and the fee in writing before you decide anything.
>
> Consultations in English and Spanish. WhatsApp welcome.

**Services to add** (each one is a searchable entry — add all of them):
Family immigration · Marriage green card · Fiancé visa · Adjustment of status · Consular
processing · Removing conditions on residence · Naturalisation and citizenship · N-400
applications · Asylum applications · Withholding of removal · Deportation defence · Removal
proceedings · Bond hearings · Immigration appeals · Work visas · Employment-based green cards
· Treaty investor visas · I-9 compliance audits · Visa refusal review · Request for evidence
responses

**Attributes to switch on:** Online appointments · Onsite services · Language assistance ·
Identifies as women-owned (if accurate) · Wheelchair accessible entrance (if accurate)

**Photos — this matters more than people expect.** Listings with photos get materially more
clicks. Upload at least: the office exterior, the building entrance, the office interior, a
professional headshot, and the logo. Real photos, not stock.

**Posts:** post once a week. Reuse the video scripts — each one is a post. Google Posts feed
into the listing and signal the profile is alive.

**Q&A:** you can ask and answer your own questions. Seed five, taken straight from
`marketing/../src/lib/content.ts`. Start with the notario question and the "does the
government call and ask for payment" one.

## 6. Also worth claiming, in this order

- **State Bar of New York** public directory — verify the listed address and firm are current

- **Bing Places** — imports from Google Business Profile in a few clicks
- **Apple Business Connect** — free, and it is what Apple Maps and Siri read
- **Nigerian diaspora directories** and DFW African business listings — small traffic each,
  but the referral quality is high and competitors are not there

## The one thing that ties it together

Every profile should push to the same two actions: **book a call** and **WhatsApp**. Not a
contact form, not "learn more". Use the same wording everywhere so it becomes recognisable.
