# Texas client acquisition — research file

Deep research (two Parallel pro-fast runs + ~30 targeted searches, Aug 2026) on how
Hogan and Crown Law acquires Texan clients. Every number carries its source. Where a
number is disputed or unverified, the file says so.

## 1. The Texas market in numbers

- Texas is **17.8% foreign-born**; immigrants are **22.2% of the labor force**.
  [American Immigration Council](https://map.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/locations/texas/)
- Largest origin groups in Texas: **Mexico 51%**, India 6%, El Salvador 5%, Vietnam
  4%, Honduras 3%. **66% of immigrants speak English well or very well.** [AIC Texas
  brief](https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/immigrants_in_texas.pdf)
- **Dallas County: ~319,000 unauthorized residents**, Mexico 55% of them. [MPI county
  profile](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/data/unauthorized-immigrant-population/county/48113)
- **North Texas has the fourth-largest unauthorized population in the country.**
  [Dallas Morning News](https://www.dallasnews.com/news/immigration/2017/02/09/north-texas-has-the-country-s-fourth-largest-population-of-unauthorized-immigrants-survey-finds/)
- **More than one million undocumented people live in Houston and Dallas combined.**
  [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/the-daily-post/one-million-undocumented-people-live-houston-dallas-combined/)
- **Dallas Immigration Court: 350,413 total cases, 3.5% grant rate.** At 1100 Commerce
  St, Suite 1060. [OpenImmigration](https://www.openimmigration.us/courts/dallas)
- **Texas immigration-court backlog: 340,827 cases**; national backlog 3,195,137
  (end-June 2026), of which ~2.31M carry formal asylum applications.
  [TRAC](https://tracreports.org/phptools/immigration/backlog)
  [TRAC quick facts](https://tracreports.org/immigration/quickfacts/eoir.html)

## 2. The single biggest demand driver right now: SB4 is in full effect

- The Fifth Circuit **lifted the block on SB4 on May 29, 2026**; the law is now
  enforced. [Fox 7 Austin](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/texas-immigration-law-sb-4-may-29-2026)
  [ILRC press release, June 2 2026](https://www.ilrc.org/about-us/press/texas%E2%80%99s-regressive-state-deportation-law-sb-4-full-effect)
- SB4 makes unauthorized entry from Mexico a **state crime** and lets local police
  arrest. [ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/fifth-circuit-rules-challenge-to-extreme-texas-immigration-law-must-be-dismissed-on-procedural-grounds)
- **Operation Lone Star since launch: 535,700+ apprehensions and referrals, 57,400
  criminal arrests, 47,500 felony charges.** [Texas DPS](https://www.dps.texas.gov/operationlonestar)
- **ICE ran a Dallas student-visa enforcement operation that arrested 27 people.**
  [ICE](https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/ice-arrests-27-during-dallas-student-visa-enforcement-operation)
- **Prairieland Detention Center, Alvarado TX** — about 40 miles SW of Dallas, the
  detention point for the DFW area. [ICE](https://www.ice.gov/detain/detention-facilities/prairieland-detention-facility)
  [KeyVisa](https://www.keyvisa.com/deportation-litigation/prairieland-ice-detention-center)
- **A 9,500-bed ICE mega-facility is proposed for Hutchins in Dallas County.** Local
  officials oppose it (Feb 2026). If it opens, Dallas becomes a detention hub.
  [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/dallas/2026/02/03/hutchins-ice-detention-center-dallas-county-texas)
  [Dallas Observer](https://www.dallasobserver.com/news/dallas-county-could-be-home-to-massive-new-ice-dentetion-facility-40630914)
- Sept 2025: a shooting at a Dallas immigration facility led ICE to move migrants to
  Prairieland — proof the DFW detention pipeline is live news.
  [KERA](https://www.keranews.org/news/2025-09-25/immigration-transfer-dallas-ice-office-prairieland-detention-center-shooting)

**Alpha:** every SB4 arrest and every detention creates a same-day bond hearing and a
family that will pay for a lawyer who answers the phone. Nobody in Dallas yet owns
"SB4 explainer" + "Prairieland bond" + "Dallas court" content as a package. That is
the fastest-possible channel to a Texan client.

## 3. The Nigerian wedge, in numbers

- **Texas: 124,717 Nigerian residents** — 19.5% of the entire Nigerian-American
  population, 2.2x the national concentration. [Neilsberg](https://www.neilsberg.com/insights/lists/nigerian-population-in-texas-by-city/)
- Top Texas cities: **Houston 22,304** (0.97%), **Dallas 7,378** (0.57%),
  **Arlington 5,261** (1.33%), Austin 3,813, **Grand Prairie 3,579** (1.80%!),
  **Fort Worth 3,575**. [Neilsberg, same]
- DFW community estimate: **50,000+ Nigerian Americans**, **16,000+ Nigerian-born in
  Dallas County**. [Settle In America](https://www.settleinamerica.com/guides/nigerian/dallas-fort-worth/)
- Houston estimates: 85,000 metro, or 150,000 first + second generation — definitions
  vary, treat as a range not a fact. [Nigeria Live 24](https://nigerialive24.com/politics/elections/houston-leads-us-cities-for-nigerian-population.html)
- Nigerian refusal-rate claim (commonly cited ~57% for B visas FY2025) is **not
  verified** in this pass — verify against the State Dept FY25 adjusted refusal table
  before using it in any material. [State Dept FY25 table](https://travel.state.gov/content/dam/visas/Statistics/Non-Immigrant-Statistics/RefusalRates/FY25.pdf)

## 8. The seven Nigeria-specific products to own

1. **K-1 fiancé petitions to Nigeria** — evidence quality + consular prep.
2. **DV selectee representation** — verify current-cycle eligibility first; do not
   market on the unverified "Nigeria is a top DV winner" premise. [DV-2026 instructions](https://travel.state.gov/content/dam/visas/Diversity-Visa/DV-Instructions-Translations/DV-2026-Instructions-Translations/DV%202026%20Plain%20Language%20Instructions%20and%20FAQs.pdf)

   rewards hardship-evidence craft. [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/family/family-of-us-citizens/provisional-unlawful-presence-waivers)
4. **Nigerian student F-1** — credibility review, funding consistency, prior refusals.
   Nigerian student enrollment dropped 25% in 2023/24; F-1 approvals are a live
   anxiety. [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/us-visa-nigerian-students-enrolment-drops-25-in-2023-24)
5. **EB-3 for Nigerian nurses** — the professional-association product; screen
   sponsorship, license, VisaScreen before accepting.
6. **Citizenship for long-term green-card holders** — travel/tax/criminal preflight.
7. **214(b) refusals** — refusal audit, changed circumstances, mock interview.

## 10. Compliance traps (Texas rules)

- Advertising must be truthful and nondeceptive (Texas Rule 7.01). No "best",
  "guaranteed", or "specialist" without a defensible basis. [Texas rules PDF](https://www.texasbar.com/Content/NavigationMenu/ForLawyers/AdvertisingReview/ADRULES2023.pdf)
- Rule 7.04(c): preapproval available from the Advertising Review Committee when
  wording is risky. [Texas Bar advertising review](https://www.texasbar.com/adreview)
- **Rule 5.04: no fee sharing with nonlawyers.** Do not pay churches, tax preparers,
  or remittance shops per referral. Flat advertising fees only.
- Describe the Nigeria admission as background and community connection, not
  authorization to practise Nigerian law in a US matter.
- Queenette is NY + Nigeria barred, not Texas barred. Federal immigration practice is
  fine (the site's position), but Texas LRIS and the Dallas Bar referral service
  generally require Texas admission — check before relying on them. NY Rule 7.1
  governs the public replies.
- SB4 content: dated updates, no immunity promises, individualized counsel only.
