## How approval works

Every post is attorney advertising under New York Rule 7.1. So the order is fixed: you
approve, then it posts. Never the other way round.

No post promises a result. No post quotes a success rate. No post answers a specific person's
case, because that answer belongs in a consultation, and every post carries the link to book
one.

You keep the approval. I keep the archive.

## What this does not do

It does not guarantee clients. Nobody can honestly promise that.

It does not replace reviews or referrals. It sits beside them.

It never pays me per lead or per case. New York Rule 5.4 bars a lawyer from a fee split with a
non-lawyer, and Rule 7.2 bars payment for a referral. A flat advertising fee is the clean way
to do this, so that is the only way I will do it.
